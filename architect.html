Zen Crypted Architect

Statement of Work — Architect (Delivery Manager / PO / CTO)

Project: Comprehensive leadership and stable continuous delivery of the x509.chat ecosystem
Position: Product Architect / Delivery Manager / Product Owner (dedicated to x509.chat)
Project context: Zen Crypted is seeking an Architect to unify product vision, technical excellence, and production coordination. This role is entirely dedicated to the x509.chat product, ensuring its stable evolution from a military-grade backend and mission-critical client into a world-class secure communications ecosystem.

Scope of Work (main deliverables):

Integration of Delivery Management, Product Ownership, and Technical Architecture:

  • Production Coordination: Manage cross-team synchronization between server and client development to ensure seamless protocol evolution and feature parity.
  • Product Responsibility: Define and refine the product roadmap for x509.chat, prioritizing features based on military and government use cases.
  • Technical Leadership: Oversee the end-to-end architecture, ensuring robust compliance with X.509, CMS, and ASN.1 standards across both server and client.

Continuous Delivery & Operational Excellence:

  • Establish and maintain a stable CI/CD pipeline for both Elixir backend and Swift iOS client.
  • Implement automated quality gates (test coverage, static analysis, security audits).
  • Manage release engineering processes, including versioning strategies and secure distribution channels.
  • Orchestrate performance benchmarking and stability testing for high-load/high-security environments.

Strategic Design & Standard Compliance:

  • Evolve the core ASN.1 schema to support next-generation messaging features.
  • Ensure cryptographic primitives and protocols remain resistant to modern and post-quantum threats.
  • Review and validate implementation of national crypto algorithms (ДСТУ) and international standards (RFCs).
  • Lead technical documentation and architectural reviews.

Management & Coordination:

  • Act as the primary technical point of contact for stakeholders.
  • Facilitate sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives for the x509.chat team.
  • Identify and mitigate technical risks and delivery bottlenecks.
  • Foster a culture of security-first development and extreme reliability.

Required skills & experience (for job/CV screening):

  • 8+ years in software engineering with at least 3 years in a technical leadership or architectural role.
  • Proven track record in Delivery Management or as a Product Owner for complex technical products.
  • Deep understanding of secure communications, PKI (X.509, CMPv2, OCSP), and cryptographic protocols (CMS, S-MIME).
  • Familiarity with the Elixir/Erlang/OTP ecosystem and Swift/iOS development.
  • Practical experience with ASN.1 (X.680) and DER encoding.
  • Experience in defense, aerospace, or government-grade secure messaging projects.
  • Exceptional communication skills in English (Fluent) and Ukrainian (Highly desirable).
  • PhD or Master degree in Computer Science or Mathematics.

Nice to have:

  • Active contributions to open-source secure messaging projects or standard bodies.
  • Experience with N2O.DEV or ERP.UNO stack.
  • Strong background in Formal Methods or high-assurance system design.

Estimated engagement & Success criteria:

  • Estimated engagement: Long-term strategic engagement (12+ months)
  • Success criteria: Predictable and stable release cycle for x509.chat; high degree of team synchronization; successful pass of external security audits and field trials.
